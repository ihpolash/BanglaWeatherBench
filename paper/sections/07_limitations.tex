\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{The equity contrast rests on ten stations.} The tropical-versus-temperate comparison uses the 10
GHCN-Daily stations in Bangladesh, giving 1{,}154 rainfall and 1{,}478 temperature windows, an order of
magnitude fewer than the BMD track. Every equity figure in this paper should be read with that $n$ attached. Three
design choices limit but do not eliminate the risk: the temperate reference is drawn from the same instrument
network; the gap-matched control is applied to models and baselines alike; and intervals come from a moving-block
bootstrap over origins rather than from window counts. We report the contrast because it is the only like-for-like
comparison available, not because ten stations settle the question.

\paragraph{One country, one climate.} Findings are specific to Bangladesh's monsoon regime. The protocol, controls
and significance machinery transfer; the numbers do not. Whether the long-lead temperature deficit appears in other
tropical networks is open.

\paragraph{Zero-shot only.} We evaluate models as they are shipped. Few-shot adaptation and light fine-tuning are out of scope; they are the
obvious next step, and cheap for several models in the roster. This paper therefore bounds the zero-shot claim
rather than the models' ceiling.

\paragraph{The roster will age.} Eight models current in 2026 will not be current for long. The harness, cached
forecasts and scoring code are released so that adding a model is a single run against unchanged windows.

\paragraph{Imputation in the source record.} The published BMD data was imputed by its authors before release. We
detect and flag suspect values and exclude flagged targets from scoring, but detection is imperfect; flagged shares
over the test period run from \SI{0.08}{\percent} (rainfall) to \SI{4.44}{\percent} (humidity), with one
station-variable at \SI{14.1}{\percent}.

\paragraph{Two comparisons are weaker than the rest.} Bright sunshine hours and downward shortwave radiation are
not the same physical quantity, so that row of the observation-versus-reanalysis contrast is reported but not
relied on. The CHIRPS dekadal task is an independent-source check at coarse resolution, not a headline.

\paragraph{Mechanisms are hypothesised, not tested.} We measure the regime reversal and the context-availability
effect; we do not identify the architectural cause of either.
